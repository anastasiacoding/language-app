French Lion: a free app to practice French

Yes, another language app, but this one is free and has no ads. Practice French at your own pace:

  • Read aloud: add any French text (a photo of a book page, a screenshot, a link or a paste), read it out loud and get a pronunciation score, with the IPA transcription of every word. How reading aloud works.
  • Translate and retell: translate the text by voice and retell it in French, with a grammar check.
  • Grammar from A1 to B2: 63 rules (articles, passé composé, imparfait, subjonctif, pronouns, si clauses and more) with endless practice. Read all French grammar rules.
  • Check genders and conjugations: le or la for 17,000 nouns, every verb in every tense.
  • Track your progress: scores, saved words and a daily streak with your lion.

Works in the browser on iPhone, Android and computers. Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play; until then, add it to your home screen as a web app (PWA): in Safari on iPhone tap Share → Add to Home Screen, in Chrome on Android tap ⋮ → Install app.
